Proven against a real scratch Postgres 16 (`scripts/apply-migrations-locally.sh`, every migration applied in order, then the verifier). Every control below was removed (the live definition mutated inside a temp copy of the verifier, a no-op mutation raising), watched to fail, and restored: **61 mutations, 61 proven** (the list is the "Red-proved controls" section). All other SQL verifiers (`scripts/verify-*.sql`, 36) also pass on the same database; the one that exits non-zero under `ON_ERROR_STOP`, `verify-discount-and-payment-structure-local.sql`, does so by design (it provokes an expected constraint error and was written to be read, not stopped).

## 2026-10-07

### What already existed (WS1, the forensic audit)
Most finance was built: invoices with milestone / change-request / maintenance kinds and a unique live invoice per milestone; payment submissions with a human-only verification (owner or the runner, `finance.verify_payment`, idempotent under the lock); receipts; refunds with approvals; expenses with categories; reconciliation with a bank import; period reports; tax period locks; GST exports; invoice reminders; the `finance` agent (generates invoices, verifies nothing). **Missing**, and so built: the financial close of a project; a structured finance exception queue (including chargeback); governed waivers; a period close with a frozen report; the finance agents' proposals with an independent reviewer; and one definition of "outstanding".

### Built (migrations `20261106100000` to `20261106500000`)
- **`20261106100000` exceptions, waivers, outstanding.** `finance.finance_exceptions` (13 kinds; chargeback and overpayment always blocking; a blocking one is resolved by someone who did not open it; a chargeback is settled by an Admin; resolved = history). `finance.waivers` (requested by Finance or Admin, decided by an Admin who is not the requester, a CHECK and the door; reduces the collectible balance; never cash). `finance.invoice_outstanding_minor` (total - net verified - approved waivers, floored at 0). `finance.sweep_finance_exceptions` (runner only: opens overdue and overpayment exceptions idempotently and closes an overdue one when the invoice stopped being overdue). `finance.observe_invoice_reminder_candidates` redefined with ONE added predicate: a fully waived invoice is not chased.
- **`20261106200000` the project financial close.** `finance.project_close_position` (computed in SQL: per milestone M1..Mn planned / invoiced / collected / refunded / net verified / waived / outstanding / overdue / unverified; aging; margin on the existing cash basis, verified revenue - (expenses + AI cost + time cost); blockers each with a reason), `finance.evaluate_project_close` (every evaluation kept), `request_close_exception` / `decide_close_exception` (a non-zero balance closes only against an approved exception that still covers it), `close_project_finances` (a person only; re-evaluates inside under the project and invoice locks; refused while anything but a balance blocks), `finance.project_is_financially_closed`, the event `project.financially_closed`. After a close, a NEW milestone invoice and a milestone-plan rewrite are refused; a non-milestone invoice (change request, service, renewal) is still allowed.
- **`20261106300000` the period close.** `finance.period_close_preview` and `close_period`: a frozen report, standing exceptions listed with reasons and formally acknowledged (door and CHECK), no overlap (door under an advisory lock and a trigger), append-only; an expense dated inside a closed period can no longer be added, changed or deleted.
- **`20261106400000` the agents.** `finance_reconciliation`, `finance_communication`, `finance_close` installed DISABLED, no tool, `moneyAuthority: 'none'`, handing back to `finance` only, verified by `quality_assurance`.
- **`20261106500000` proposals.** `finance_agent_requests`, `finance_proposals`, `finance_proposal_decisions`; the service-only `record_finance_proposal` refuses another organization, the wrong agent or kind, a secret, evidence that does not exist in the organization and project, a finding with no evidence, and for a reminder: nothing owed, an amount that is not the database's balance, a claim that payment was received, a promised discount / waiver / refund / deferral / plan, and account-number-shaped text. `accept_finance_proposal` / `reject_finance_proposal` need a Finance person or Admin who did not request the run; accepting a reminder draft sends nothing and is refused when the balance it quotes has changed; accepting an exception classification opens the exception through the existing door as the accepting person.

### Defects found by driving it
1. A same-statement check that called a door and read its effect saw the pre-call snapshot (three times); split into two statements.
2. `resolve_finance_exception` answered `admin_only` before `self_resolution` when Finance resolved its own chargeback; the order was swapped so the creator rule is reported first.
3. The first red-proof of the period cash figure was green: it mutated the aggregate but not the per-project subquery, and the verifier only asserted the project that had no unverified payment. A project with an unverified payment in the period was added to the verifier; the mutation then went red.
4. The first red-proof of "a stale draft is refused" was green: the invoice became `paid`, so the status test refused it first. The fixture was changed to a partial payment so only the balance comparison can refuse it.
5. A regex for "account-number-shaped text" first flagged an ordinary invoice number (`INV-2026-000123`); it now needs nine consecutive digits or a card grouping.

### Decisions kept
- The service-role exemption in `finance.verify_payment*` is a documented owner decision and was neither widened nor narrowed; the verifier shows Finance still gets `forbidden`.
- A waiver does not change the invoice status or `paid_minor` / `verified_minor`: the invoice is history and the waiver is a separate record. The M3 / M4 "verified in full" gates are unchanged, so a waived balance does not open a payment gate; a gate stays a person's act.
- A refund re-opens the balance (net verified falls), so a refunded-and-not-voided invoice blocks the close until a person deals with it. This is deliberate (an auditable adjustment), and the verifier asserts it.
- The margin is the report's existing cash-basis definition (verified revenue less expenses, AI cost and time cost), tax-inclusive as invoiced; one margin definition, not two.
- The sweep needs a caller; nothing schedules it (see manual actions).
