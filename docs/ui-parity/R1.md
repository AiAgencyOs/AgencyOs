## Notes and things a reader might trip over

- `20261005100000` also adds the missing tenancy guard for `projects.tasks.parent_task_id` (left unguarded by `20261004100000`; `core.unguarded_org_fks()` listed it). Additive and idempotent.
- The pre-existing `scripts/verify-tenancy-guards.mjs` still reports `sales.quotation_clauses` (org not frozen) and another stream's `crm.lead_files.carried_to_project_id`; neither is this stream's.
- A department is read by an owner / ops admin on the Team tab only. The org-level roster in Settings does not show it yet.
- Closing a sprint moves nothing: its tasks keep it, and the audit row records how many were not done. No capacity, velocity or burndown exists, by decision.
- Owner decision 8 leaves "Waiting on you" as a badge only; if the owner wants the filter chip back it is one entry in `TABS` on the Discussion page.
- `scripts/verify-kit-r1.mjs` is the shared plumbing of the five verifiers (target, minted JWTs, fixtures, cleanup).
